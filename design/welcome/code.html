Welcome to the Playing Socrates Study

An exploration of AI-assisted learning and Socratic inquiry.

Thank you for participating in this research study. We are investigating how generative AI can be utilized not just as an answer engine, but as a pedagogical partner that guides students through complex topics using the Socratic method.

psychology

The Goal

Our hypothesis is that guided inquiry—prompting you to think critically rather than providing direct answers—leads to deeper conceptual understanding in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) theory.

Over the next sequence of steps, you will complete a brief pre-test, learn how to interact with the AI tutor, engage in a study session focused on HCI concepts, and conclude with a post-test and feedback survey.

Estimated time to complete: 45-60 minutes.
