Step 3 of 9: Consent

Informed Consent

Please read the following information carefully before proceeding with the study.

1. Study Purpose

The purpose of this research study is to investigate the efficacy of AI-assisted Socratic dialogue in enhancing the comprehension of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) theoretical frameworks. You are being asked to participate because you are currently enrolled in an introductory or intermediate HCI course.

2. Procedures

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to complete a series of online modules that include:

  • A brief pre-test assessing your baseline knowledge.
  • Interactive reading materials on HCI theory.
  • A guided practice session with an AI acting as a Socratic tutor.
  • A post-test to evaluate knowledge acquisition.
  • A short feedback survey regarding your experience.

The entire process is designed to take approximately 45-60 minutes to complete.

3. Risks and Discomforts

There are no anticipated physical risks associated with this study. You may experience mild fatigue or frustration typical of engaging with academic coursework or novel digital interfaces. You are free to take breaks or withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.

4. Benefits

While there may be no direct immediate benefit to you, your participation will contribute to the broader understanding of how conversational AI can be integrated into educational environments to support active learning methodologies.

5. Confidentiality and Data Handling

All data collected during this study will be kept strictly confidential. Your responses will be anonymized and assigned a participant ID (e.g., Participant-204). The AI interaction transcripts will be stored on secure, encrypted servers accessible only to the primary research team. Data will be retained for a period of three years post-publication, after which it will be permanently destroyed.
